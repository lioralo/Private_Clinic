## Overview

Calm, clinic-focused design with Sanctuary teal primary, ample whitespace, and minimal motion (shadow/border feedback only — no hover lifts).

## Style Foundations

- **Visual style:** minimal, calm, admin-friendly
- **Typography scale:** 12/14/16/20/24/32
- **Typography fonts:** primary=Roboto, display=Poppins, mono=Inconsolata, Hebrew=Heebo
- **Color palette:** teal primary, slate neutrals, semantic success/warning/danger
- **Spacing scale:** 8pt baseline grid
- **Motion:** transitions on color, background, border, box-shadow only

## Colors

- **Primary (#134e4a):** Sanctuary teal — main brand, buttons, active nav tint
- **Primary dark (#0f766e):** Hover states
- **Secondary (#64748B):** Secondary text and actions
- **Success (#16A34A):** Positive states
- **Warning (#D97706):** Caution states
- **Danger (#DC2626):** Errors and destructive actions
- **Surface (#FFFFFF):** Cards and panels
- **Background (#f7f9fb):** Page background
- **Text (#111827):** Primary text
